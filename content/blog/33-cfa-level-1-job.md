Passing Level I does not make you a charterholder, and no employer treats it as one. But it changes more at work than many people expect, because it proves three things at once: you can learn a large body of material, you can do it alongside work or study, and you now speak the language of investment teams.

[[figure]]

## What the data says

CFA Institute's 2025 study of more than 100 finance employers in India, run by Kantar, gives the clearest employer view:

- the CFA Program is **highly valued for entry- and mid-level investment roles**, while senior positions put relevant experience first;
- employers say the designation gives early-career candidates **an immediate credibility boost** and often **fast-tracks them into investment roles**;
- the top reasons hiring managers give are **technical depth**, **global recognition** and **integrity**, and 47% describe CFA candidates as "plug-and-play" for roles such as credit analyst, equity researcher and risk associate;
- 54% of employers look for candidates who combine an MBA with the CFA charter.

Raises and promotions also depend on performance and timing, so read these as typical outcomes, not promises.

## What Level I changes in your current job

- **You follow the conversation.** Discounted cash flows, duration, accruals and portfolio risk stop being jargon.
- **You can take on analytical work:** building a valuation, checking a credit, explaining a portfolio's risk.
- **It signals ambition to your manager.** Many firms consider exam progress in promotions and in who joins investment projects.
- **It earns employer support** for the next levels: fees, materials and study leave.

## Jobs that Level I opens

Level I is most useful for **entry and early-career roles**, especially if you are switching into finance:

- research associate or junior analyst (equity or credit research);
- investment or portfolio analyst at an asset manager;
- risk analyst, performance analyst, fund analyst;
- wealth management associate, relationship support;
- financial analyst in corporate finance or treasury;
- operations and data roles at asset managers and index providers, a common route into investment teams.

For senior investment roles, employers look for Level III or the charter plus experience.

## How to describe it on your CV and LinkedIn

CFA Institute's Standards of Professional Conduct (Standard VII(B)) set strict rules on how candidates may refer to the program. The safe wording is a statement of fact about the exam you passed:

- **Correct:** "Passed Level I of the CFA Program" or "Level II candidate in the CFA Program".
- **Not allowed:** "CFA Level I", "CFA (Level I)", "CFA candidate (pending)", or anything that suggests a partial designation.

Only charterholders may put "CFA" after their name. Getting this right matters: employers in the industry notice.

## How to make the most of it

1. **Tell your manager** and ask for analytical work that uses what you learned.
2. **Apply the topics:** volunteer to build a model or review a report.
3. **Register for Level II** while the material is fresh; momentum is valuable.
4. **Combine it with a practical skill** such as Excel modelling, Python or SQL. Level I plus a skill employers can see is stronger than Level I alone.
5. **Network** through your local CFA society events, which candidates can usually attend.

## Other ways to show investment knowledge early

If Level I is too big a first step, CFA Institute's shorter certificates can show interest and knowledge, for example the Investment Foundations certificate. See [CIPM and other CFA Institute programs](blog-cipm-and-cfa-institute-programs.html).

## Questions people ask

### Can I get a job after CFA Level I?

Yes, typically in junior analyst, research associate, risk, fund analysis or wealth support roles, especially when combined with internships and practical skills.

### Does CFA Level I increase salary?

Passing Level I rarely brings a raise on its own; pay depends mainly on the role and employer. What it does, according to employers in CFA Institute's India study, is make early-career candidates more credible and speed their move into investment roles, where pay is higher.

### How do I write CFA Level I on my resume?

Write "Passed Level I of the CFA Program". Do not write "CFA Level I" or use "CFA" after your name; only charterholders can do that.

### Is CFA Level I worth it on its own?

It is worth it as a step: it builds knowledge and signals commitment. Its full value comes from continuing to Level II and III.
