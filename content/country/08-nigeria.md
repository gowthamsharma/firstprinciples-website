Nigeria has one of the fastest-growing CFA communities in Africa. The exams were first held in the country in June 2018, when about 870 candidates sat them in Lagos, and computer-based testing now runs in Lagos and Abuja. Charterholders work in asset management, pension fund administration, banking, stockbroking and private equity.

[[figure]]

## Where to take the CFA exams in Nigeria

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names Lagos and Abuja. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in NGN

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use about ₦1,363 per US dollar (official market rate, 19 November 2026) and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (NGN) | Standard (USD) | Standard (NGN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | ₦1.55 million | USD 1,490 | ₦2.03 million |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | ₦1.55 million | USD 1,490 | ₦2.03 million |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | ₦1.69 million | USD 1,590 | ₦2.17 million |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **₦4.80 million** | **USD 4,570** | **₦6.23 million** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- Salary data for Nigeria predate the naira's fall, so we do not compare fees with pay there.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in Nigeria

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in Nigeria is **₦6 million** a year (about US$4,000), based on 36 (a small sample) profiles, last updated October 2023. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with [CFA salaries in 19 countries](blog-cfa-salary.html) and [pay by job](blog-cfa-jobs-salaries.html).

## Who hires charterholders in Nigeria

- **Access (Access Holdings group)**: 82 candidates, 11 charterholders

In CFA Society Nigeria's membership data (June 2026), Access employed the most CFA candidates in Nigeria and the second-largest number of charterholders. Pension fund administrators, asset managers, the large commercial banks and investment banks are the other main employers.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in Nigeria.

## How the charter is recognised in Nigeria

- **No regulator waiver.** Nigerian regulators do not waive licensing requirements for charterholders, but CFA Society Nigeria works with the CBN, SEC, PenCom, NAICOM and NDIC on investor education and professional standards.
- **Employers.** Asset managers, pension fund administrators and banks value the charter for investment and research roles.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

CFA Society Nigeria was founded in 2013. It runs the Research Challenge, an Ethics Challenge, investment conferences and a weekly financial literacy radio show, Money Talks. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## Fees in naira

The naira has moved sharply since 2023, so the naira cost of the exams changes from month to month. At about ₦1,363 per dollar (the official market rate in mid-November 2026), Level I at early registration costs about ₦1.55 million. Check whether your bank card supports dollar payments and what rate it applies.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in Nigeria?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about ₦1.55 million, and all three levels USD 3,520, about ₦4.80 million at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Where can I take the CFA exam in Nigeria?

In Lagos and Abuja.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
