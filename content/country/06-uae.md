The UAE has become one of the fastest-growing places to hold the charter. Sovereign wealth funds and government-related investors in Abu Dhabi, banks and family offices across the country, and the asset managers in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) and Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) all hire for investment roles where the charter is valued.

[[figure]]

## Where to take the CFA exams in the United Arab Emirates

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names Dubai and Abu Dhabi. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in AED

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use the fixed peg of AED 3.6725 per US dollar and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (AED) | Standard (USD) | Standard (AED) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | AED 4,190 | USD 1,490 | AED 5,470 |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | AED 4,190 | USD 1,490 | AED 5,470 |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | AED 4,550 | USD 1,590 | AED 5,840 |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **AED 12,900** | **USD 4,570** | **AED 16,800** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **4 weeks** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in the UAE.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in the United Arab Emirates

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in the UAE is **AED 175,000** a year (about US$48,000), based on 113 profiles, last updated September 2025. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with [CFA salaries in 19 countries](blog-cfa-salary.html) and [pay by job](blog-cfa-jobs-salaries.html).

## Who hires charterholders in the United Arab Emirates

Sovereign wealth funds, government-related investors, banks, family offices and international asset managers in the DIFC and ADGM are the main employers. Packages often add housing and schooling allowances, and there is no personal income tax.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in the UAE.

## How the charter is recognised in the United Arab Emirates

- **Licensing equivalence (new in 2026).** On 8 October 2026 the UAE Capital Market Authority (formerly the Securities and Commodities Authority) approved recognising the CFA Program as equivalent to licensing exams from the Chartered Institute for Securities & Investment (CISI): Level I to CISI's Fundamentals of Financial Services and Global Securities Operations, Level II to the International Certificate in Wealth Management, and Level III (Private Markets pathway) to the Corporate Finance Technical Foundation and ICWIM Level 3.
- **Free zones.** The DIFC (regulated by the DFSA) and ADGM (regulated by the FSRA) set their own competence rules; check them for your role.
- **Employers.** Many investment roles in Abu Dhabi and Dubai list the charter as required or preferred.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

CFA Society Emirates runs events, study support and continuing education in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## Fees in dirhams

Because the dirham is pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725, the dirham cost of the exams only changes when CFA Institute changes its fees.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in the UAE?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about AED 4,190, and all three levels USD 3,520, about AED 12,900 at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Is the CFA recognised in the UAE?

Yes. In October 2026 the UAE Capital Market Authority approved treating CFA Program levels as equivalent to specific CISI licensing exams used in UAE licensing, and many employers list the charter in job adverts.

### What is the CFA salary in Dubai?

The average base salary reported by people with the CFA is about AED 175,000, before allowances. Housing and schooling allowances and the absence of income tax make packages larger than the base suggests.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
