Singapore is one of Asia's two big asset-management centres, home to regional headquarters of global managers, two state investors (GIC and Temasek) and a fast-growing wealth management industry. The charter is common among its investment professionals and expected in many buy-side roles.

[[figure]]

## Where to take the CFA exams in Singapore

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names Singapore. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in SGD

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use European Central Bank reference rates of 20 November 2026 (1 USD = 1.2884 SGD) and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (SGD) | Standard (USD) | Standard (SGD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | S$1,470 | USD 1,490 | S$1,920 |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | S$1,470 | USD 1,490 | S$1,920 |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | S$1,600 | USD 1,590 | S$2,050 |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **S$4,540** | **USD 4,570** | **S$5,890** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **2 weeks** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in Singapore.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in Singapore

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in Singapore is **S$115,000** a year (about US$89,000), based on 131 profiles, last updated July 2025. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with [CFA salaries in 19 countries](blog-cfa-salary.html) and [pay by job](blog-cfa-jobs-salaries.html).

## Who hires charterholders in Singapore

- **DBS**: 305 charterholders

DBS is the Singapore-headquartered bank with the most charterholders in CFA Institute's 2025 list. Global managers with regional hubs in Singapore, the state investors and private banks are major employers too.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in Singapore.

## How the charter is recognised in Singapore

- **CMFAS modules.** CFA Institute's recognition fact card lists an exemption from several Capital Markets and Financial Advisory Services (CMFAS) exam modules for charterholders seeking a licence as a fund manager or financial adviser representative. MAS revised its competency rules from April 2024, so confirm the current position with MAS or your employer's compliance team.
- **Employers.** Asset managers, private banks and the state investors often list the charter as preferred or required for investment roles.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

CFA Society Singapore runs events, career support and continuing education. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## Bonuses matter

In Singapore a large part of investment pay comes as a bonus; a typical portfolio manager's bonus is a quarter to a third of base pay. Compare total compensation, not base salary alone.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in Singapore?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about S$1,470, and all three levels USD 3,520, about S$4,540 at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Does the CFA exempt me from CMFAS exams?

CFA Institute lists exemptions from several CMFAS modules for charterholders, but MAS changed its competency framework in 2024. Check the current MAS notice for your licence type before relying on an exemption.

### What is the CFA salary in Singapore?

The average base salary reported by people with the CFA is about S$115,000, before bonuses.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
