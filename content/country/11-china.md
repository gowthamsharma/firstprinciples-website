Mainland China became one of the CFA Program's largest sources of candidates in the 2010s, and CFA Institute opened its first mainland office in 2015. Candidates work at securities firms, fund houses, banks and insurers, and many study while at university.

[[figure]]

## Where to take the CFA exams in mainland China

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names 34 cities, including Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Chengdu, Wuhan, Xi'an, Tianjin, Suzhou and Xiamen. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in CNY

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use European Central Bank reference rates of 20 November 2026 (1 USD = 6.6777 CNY) and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (CNY) | Standard (USD) | Standard (CNY) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | ¥7,610 | USD 1,490 | ¥9,950 |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | ¥7,610 | USD 1,490 | ¥9,950 |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | ¥8,280 | USD 1,590 | ¥10,600 |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **¥23,500** | **USD 4,570** | **¥30,500** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **4 weeks** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in mainland China.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in mainland China

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in mainland China is **¥300,000** a year (about US$45,000), based on 15 (a very small sample) profiles, last updated April 2023. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with [CFA salaries in 19 countries](blog-cfa-salary.html) and [pay by job](blog-cfa-jobs-salaries.html).

## Who hires charterholders in mainland China

- **Bank of China**: 509 charterholders
- **ICBC**: 387 charterholders
- **China Merchants**: 357 charterholders

Bank of China, ICBC and China Merchants are the mainland-headquartered groups in CFA Institute's 2025 list of firms with 300 or more charterholders. Securities firms and fund management companies in Shanghai, Beijing and Shenzhen are the other major employers.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in mainland China.

## How the charter is recognised in mainland China

- **No regulator waiver.** Chinese licensing exams for securities and fund practitioners are separate; the charter does not replace them.
- **Employers.** Large financial groups, securities firms and fund houses value the charter for research and investment roles.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

There are CFA societies in Beijing, Shanghai and Shenzhen (the Shenzhen society was launched in 2017). Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## Paying the fees

Fees are charged in US dollars, so pay with a card that supports international payments, and check your bank's limits on foreign-currency spending before registration day.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in mainland China?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about ¥7,610, and all three levels USD 3,520, about ¥23,500 at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Where can I take the CFA exam in China?

In 34 mainland cities, including Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Chengdu and Wuhan.

### Is the CFA useful in China?

Yes for investment careers at securities firms, fund houses, banks and insurers, and for roles with international firms. It does not replace Chinese practitioner licensing exams.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
