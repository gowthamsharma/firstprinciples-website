London is Europe's largest asset-management centre, and the charter is commonly listed in its buy-side job adverts. UK candidates often pair it with the Investment Management Certificate (IMC), the entry-level exam many firms ask graduates to pass.

## Where to take the CFA exams in the United Kingdom

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names 13 cities: London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Liverpool, Newcastle, Bristol, Southampton, Peterborough, Cardiff, Edinburgh, Glasgow and Belfast. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in GBP

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use European Central Bank reference rates of 20 November 2026 (1 USD = 0.76192 GBP) and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (GBP) | Standard (USD) | Standard (GBP) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | £870 | USD 1,490 | £1,140 |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | £870 | USD 1,490 | £1,140 |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | £940 | USD 1,590 | £1,210 |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **£2,680** | **USD 4,570** | **£3,480** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **2 weeks** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in the UK.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in the United Kingdom

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in the UK is **£61,000** a year (about US$80,000), based on 210 profiles, last updated March 2026. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Global firms with large London offices, such as BlackRock, JPMorgan, UBS and Goldman Sachs, are among the biggest employers of charterholders worldwide.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in the UK.

## How the charter is recognised in the United Kingdom

- **Academic level.** UK NARIC (now Ecctis) benchmarked the CFA charter and Level III at Level 7 of the UK framework, the level of a master's degree. Level II is benchmarked at Level 6 (bachelor's) and Level I at Level 5.
- **FCA qualification routes.** For retail investment advice, the FCA recognises routes that combine the CFA with the Investment Management Certificate (IMC), sometimes with gap-fill study; check the FCA's current list of appropriate qualifications for your role.
- **Employers.** London buy-side firms often list the charter as preferred for analyst and portfolio roles; many pay for the exams.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

CFA Society United Kingdom (CFA UK) is one of the largest societies in the world. It runs the IMC exam, career events and continuing education in London and online. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## CFA or IMC first?

If you are starting in a UK investment firm, your employer may ask for the IMC first because it covers UK regulation. The IMC is shorter and cheaper; the charter is far broader and globally portable. Many people do the IMC in their first year, then start Level I.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in the UK?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about £870, and all three levels USD 3,520, about £2,680 at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Is the CFA equivalent to a master's degree in the UK?

Ecctis (formerly UK NARIC) benchmarks the charter at Level 7, comparable to a master's degree. That is an academic comparison, not a degree: universities decide for themselves whether to give credit.

### Where can I sit the CFA exam in the UK?

In 13 cities including London, Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Cardiff and Belfast.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
