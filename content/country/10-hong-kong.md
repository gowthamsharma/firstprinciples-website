Hong Kong has one of Asia's largest communities of charterholders. It is the gateway for international money into mainland China and home to the Asian headquarters of many global banks and asset managers.

[[figure]]

## Where to take the CFA exams in Hong Kong

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names Hong Kong. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in HKD

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use European Central Bank reference rates of 20 November 2026 (1 USD = 7.8389 HKD) and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (HKD) | Standard (USD) | Standard (HKD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | HK$8,940 | USD 1,490 | HK$11,700 |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | HK$8,940 | USD 1,490 | HK$11,700 |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | HK$9,720 | USD 1,590 | HK$12,500 |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **HK$27,600** | **USD 4,570** | **HK$35,800** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **2 weeks** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in Hong Kong.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in Hong Kong

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in Hong Kong is **HK$796,000** a year (about US$102,000), based on 78 profiles, last updated October 2025. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with [CFA salaries in 19 countries](blog-cfa-salary.html) and [pay by job](blog-cfa-jobs-salaries.html).

## Who hires charterholders in Hong Kong

- **HSBC**: 1,424 worldwide
- **Standard Chartered**: 474 worldwide

Global banks and asset managers with Asian headquarters in Hong Kong, mainland Chinese securities firms and fund houses, and private banks are the main employers.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in Hong Kong.

## How the charter is recognised in Hong Kong

- **SFC licensing.** CFA Institute's recognition fact card lists the Securities and Futures Commission recognising Level I as a recognised industry qualification for licensed representatives, with some licensing exam papers waived, and the charter as a recognised professional qualification for responsible officers. Confirm the details in the SFC's current Guidelines on Competence.
- **Employers.** Many asset-management and research roles list the charter as required or preferred.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

CFA Society Hong Kong is one of the largest and most active societies in Asia. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## The dollar peg

The Hong Kong dollar trades in a narrow band around 7.8 per US dollar, so the local cost of the exams barely changes.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in Hong Kong?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about HK$8,940, and all three levels USD 3,520, about HK$27,600 at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Does the CFA help with SFC licensing?

CFA Institute lists Level I as a recognised industry qualification for SFC licensed representatives and the charter as a recognised qualification for responsible officers. Check the SFC's current Guidelines on Competence for your licence type.

### What is the CFA salary in Hong Kong?

The average base salary reported by people with the CFA is about HK$796,000, before bonuses, which are a large part of pay in Hong Kong.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
