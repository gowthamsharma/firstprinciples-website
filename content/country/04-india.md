India is one of the CFA Program's largest and fastest-growing markets. Candidates come from commerce, engineering and chartered accountancy, and many work in Mumbai's asset managers and brokers or in the research, valuation and analytics centres of global banks in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Gurugram and Pune.

## Where to take the CFA exams in India

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names 30 cities, from Mumbai, New Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata and Pune to Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Kochi, Indore, Guwahati, Surat and Visakhapatnam. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in INR

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use European Central Bank reference rates of 20 November 2026 (1 USD = 92.68 INR) and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (INR) | Standard (USD) | Standard (INR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | ₹1.06 lakh | USD 1,490 | ₹1.38 lakh |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | ₹1.06 lakh | USD 1,490 | ₹1.38 lakh |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | ₹1.15 lakh | USD 1,590 | ₹1.47 lakh |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **₹3.26 lakh** | **USD 4,570** | **₹4.24 lakh** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **17 weeks** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in India.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in India

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in India is **₹10 lakh** a year (about US$11,000), based on 394 profiles, last updated March 2026. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with [CFA salaries in 19 countries](blog-cfa-salary.html) and [pay by job](blog-cfa-jobs-salaries.html).

## Who hires charterholders in India

Global banks and the Big Four run large research, risk and valuation teams in India; worldwide, EY, PwC, Deloitte and KPMG together employ about 4,000 charterholders. In CFA Institute's India talent report, 54% of employers said they seek candidates who combine an MBA with the CFA charter.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in India.

## How the charter is recognised in India

- **No regulator waiver.** SEBI's qualification rules for investment advisers and research analysts are met through a degree plus NISM certifications; the CFA charter does not replace the NISM exams.
- **Employers value it.** In CFA Institute's 2026 India study, charterholders awarded in 2024–2025 (about six years' experience on average) reported pay of about ₹32 lakh a year, and all charterholders about ₹67 lakh.
- **Exam access.** India has 30 test-centre cities, more than any country except the United States and mainland China.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

CFA Society India is based in Mumbai and runs events, study support and the Research Challenge across the country. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## What the fees mean in rupees

At early registration the three exams cost about ₹3.3 lakh at today's rate, before study materials. Card payments in dollars add a foreign-currency markup, and banks may add tax on foreign card spending, so check the final charge with your bank. The Access Scholarship (below) is worth applying for if the fees are a stretch.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in India?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about ₹1.06 lakh, and all three levels USD 3,520, about ₹3.26 lakh at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Is the CFA recognised in India?

Employers recognise it widely, especially in asset management, equity research, investment banking and risk. It is not a legal licence: SEBI-registered advisers and analysts still need NISM certifications.

### Where are CFA exam centres in India?

In 30 cities, including Mumbai, New Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad and Jaipur.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
