The United States is where the charter began in 1963 and where the most charterholders still work. Asset managers, banks, pension funds and insurers in New York, Boston, Chicago and San Francisco list it in job adverts for research, portfolio management and risk roles, and in most states it also replaces the investment adviser licensing exam.

[[figure]]

## Where to take the CFA exams in the United States

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names more than 180 cities, in every state, from New York, Chicago, Boston and San Francisco to smaller cities such as Boise, Billings and Anchorage. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in USD

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. 

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (USD) | Standard (USD) | Standard (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | $1,140 | USD 1,490 | $1,490 |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | $1,140 | USD 1,490 | $1,490 |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | $1,240 | USD 1,590 | $1,590 |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **$3,520** | **USD 4,570** | **$4,570** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **2 weeks** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in the US.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in the United States

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in the US is **$110,000** a year (about US$110,000), based on 1,826 profiles, last updated July 2026. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

## How the charter is recognised in the United States

- **Investment adviser licensing.** Charterholders receive a waiver from the Uniform Investment Adviser Law Examination (Series 65) in the states that require a licensing exam for investment adviser representatives (NASAA and CFA Institute).
- **Research analysts.** A research analyst who has passed Levels I and II may request an exemption from FINRA's Series 86 (the analysis part of the research analyst exam); Series 87 is still required.
- **Employers.** There is no legal requirement to hold the charter, but many buy-side job adverts list it as preferred or required for senior research and portfolio roles.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

The United States has dozens of local CFA societies, from CFA Society New York and CFA Society Chicago to CFA Society Los Angeles and CFA Society Boston. They run study groups, mock exams, networking events and the CFA Institute Research Challenge for university teams. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## Taxes and employer support

Many US employers reimburse exam fees and study materials, often after you pass. If you pay yourself, CFA exam fees may or may not be deductible depending on your situation and current tax law; ask a tax adviser before assuming either way.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in the US?

USD 1,140 for Level I or II and USD 1,240 for Level III at early registration, USD 3,520 for all three. At the standard rate the total is USD 4,570. Study materials are extra.

### Does the CFA replace the Series 65?

In the states that require the Series 65 exam for investment adviser representatives, charterholders can have it waived. You still need to register with your state, and broker-dealer roles still need the FINRA exams.

### How many CFA test centres are there in the US?

CFA Institute's planned list names more than 180 US cities. Seat availability varies by window, so book early if you need a particular centre.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
