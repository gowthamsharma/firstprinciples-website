Australia's compulsory superannuation system has built one of the world's largest pools of pension assets, and the funds and asset managers that invest it, mostly in Sydney and Melbourne, are the main employers of charterholders.

[[figure]]

## Where to take the CFA exams in Australia

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names seven cities: Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Canberra and Hobart. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in AUD

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use European Central Bank reference rates of 20 November 2026 (1 USD = 1.5002 AUD) and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (AUD) | Standard (USD) | Standard (AUD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | A$1,710 | USD 1,490 | A$2,240 |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | A$1,710 | USD 1,490 | A$2,240 |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | A$1,860 | USD 1,590 | A$2,390 |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **A$5,280** | **USD 4,570** | **A$6,860** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **2 weeks** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in Australia.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in Australia

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in Australia is **A$119,000** a year (about US$79,000), based on 116 profiles, last updated November 2025. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with [CFA salaries in 19 countries](blog-cfa-salary.html) and [pay by job](blog-cfa-jobs-salaries.html).

## Who hires charterholders in Australia

- **Macquarie**: 388 charterholders

Macquarie is the Australian-headquartered firm with the most charterholders in CFA Institute's 2025 list. Superannuation funds, asset managers and the big four banks are the other major employers.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in Australia.

## How the charter is recognised in Australia

- **Financial advice.** CFA Institute's fact card describes a route combining Level I with RG 146 gap training for retail financial advice. Australia replaced RG 146 with new education standards for advisers in 2019, so check ASIC's current requirements and approved qualifications.
- **Employers.** Super funds and asset managers often list the charter as preferred for investment roles.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

CFA Societies Australia runs events and continuing education across the major cities. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## Fees in Australian dollars

Fees are charged in US dollars, so the Australian-dollar cost moves with the exchange rate.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in Australia?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about A$1,710, and all three levels USD 3,520, about A$5,280 at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Where can I take the CFA exam in Australia?

In Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Canberra and Hobart.

### What is the CFA salary in Australia?

The average base salary reported by people with the CFA is about A$119,000, before bonuses and superannuation contributions.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
