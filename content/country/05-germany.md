In Germany the charter is most useful in Frankfurt and Munich: at asset managers, banks, insurers, index and data providers, and the central banks in Frankfurt. It is an English-language qualification in a market where much of the work is done in German, so it signals international readiness as well as technical depth.

[[figure]]

## Where to take the CFA exams in Germany

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names six cities: Frankfurt, Munich, Berlin, Hamburg, Düsseldorf and Stuttgart. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in EUR

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use European Central Bank reference rates of 20 November 2026 (1 USD = 0.90992 EUR) and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (EUR) | Standard (USD) | Standard (EUR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | €1,040 | USD 1,490 | €1,360 |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | €1,040 | USD 1,490 | €1,360 |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | €1,130 | USD 1,590 | €1,450 |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **€3,200** | **USD 4,570** | **€4,160** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **2 weeks** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in Germany.

See [2027 exam dates and fees](cfa-dates-fees.html) for every window and deadline, and [the total cost of the CFA](blog-cfa-total-cost.html) for materials and retakes.

## What people with the CFA earn in Germany

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in Germany is **€76,000** a year (about US$84,000), based on 52 profiles, last updated September 2025. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with [CFA salaries in 19 countries](blog-cfa-salary.html) and [pay by job](blog-cfa-jobs-salaries.html).

## Who hires charterholders in Germany

- **Allianz (including PIMCO and Allianz Global Investors)**: 1,011 charterholders
- **Deutsche Bank (including DWS)**: 759 charterholders

Insurers, savings and cooperative bank groups, index and data providers, and the ECB and Bundesbank also employ charterholders in Frankfurt.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in Germany.

## How the charter is recognised in Germany

- **No general licence.** German rules for investment advisers (the Sachkunde requirement) set out their own accepted qualifications and experience; check with your employer's compliance team whether the charter counts for your role.
- **Deutsche Börse.** CFA Institute's recognition fact card lists Deutsche Börse accepting a pass at Level III as meeting the professional requirement to be an exchange trader.
- **Employers.** Frankfurt asset managers and banks often name the charter as preferred for portfolio management and research.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

CFA Society Germany is based in Frankfurt and runs events, study support and continuing education in German and English. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## Studying alongside a German job

Most German candidates study alongside full-time work. Plan around the summer holiday and Christmas, and consider the August or November windows, which leave room for a quieter study period beforehand.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in Germany?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about €1,040, and all three levels USD 3,520, about €3,200 at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Is the CFA recognised in Germany?

It is widely known among asset managers, banks and insurers, especially in Frankfurt and Munich, but it is not a legal licence for retail investment advice.

### Where can I take the CFA exam in Germany?

In Frankfurt, Munich, Berlin, Hamburg, Düsseldorf and Stuttgart.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
