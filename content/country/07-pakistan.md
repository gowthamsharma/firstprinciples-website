In Pakistan the charter is a strong signal precisely because it is still rare. Charterholders work in Karachi's asset management companies, brokerages and banks, at the Pakistan Stock Exchange and regulators, and increasingly for Gulf employers who recruit from Pakistan.

[[figure]]

## Where to take the CFA exams in Pakistan

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names four cities: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Multan. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

## What the CFA costs in PKR

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use about Rs 276.6 per US dollar (interbank rate, November 2026) and are rounded.

| Exam | Early (USD) | Early (PKR) | Standard (USD) | Standard (PKR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level I | USD 1,140 | Rs 315,300 | USD 1,490 | Rs 412,100 |
| Level II | USD 1,140 | Rs 315,300 | USD 1,490 | Rs 412,100 |
| Level III | USD 1,240 | Rs 343,000 | USD 1,590 | Rs 439,800 |
| **All three levels** | **USD 3,520** | **Rs 973,600** | **USD 4,570** | **Rs 1,264,000** |

- There is **no enrolment fee** any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
- Rescheduling within your window costs **USD 250**. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
- Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
- At early registration, the three exams together cost about **12 months** of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in Pakistan.

## What people with the CFA earn in Pakistan

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in Pakistan is **Rs 1.0 million** a year (about US$4,000), based on 42 (a small sample) profiles, last updated July 2026. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with [CFA salaries in 19 countries](blog-cfa-salary.html) and [pay by job](blog-cfa-jobs-salaries.html).

## Who hires charterholders in Pakistan

Asset management companies, commercial and investment banks, brokerages, insurers and development finance institutions are the main employers. Many Pakistani charterholders build careers in the Gulf, where the charter carries over directly.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in Pakistan.

## How the charter is recognised in Pakistan

- **SECP research analyst exemption.** The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan exempts charterholders from the mandatory research analyst certification, provided they pass the Pakistan Market Regulations (PMR) certification.
- **Employers.** Asset management companies and brokerages frequently list the charter or progress in the program in job adverts.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

## The local CFA society

CFA Society Pakistan, based in Karachi with activity in Lahore and Islamabad, has been active for more than 20 years. It works with the SECP and runs charter award ceremonies, events and scholarships. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

## Fees in rupees

At about Rs 276.6 per dollar, Level I at early registration costs about Rs 315,000. That is close to four months of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in Pakistan, which is why the Access Scholarship matters so much here.

## Scholarships

CFA Institute's **Access Scholarship** reduces the exam fee to **USD 400** for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the **November 2027** exams opens on **9 February 2027** and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

## Questions people ask

### How much does the CFA cost in Pakistan?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about Rs 315,300, and all three levels USD 3,520, about Rs 973,600 at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

### Where can I take the CFA exam in Pakistan?

In Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Multan.

### Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
