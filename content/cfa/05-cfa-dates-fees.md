- The one-time **enrolment fee was abolished** on 29 April 2025 (it was USD 350).
- These fees apply to exams sat from February 2026. CFA Institute has not announced different fees for 2027 at the time of writing.
- Moving your appointment **within** the same window costs **USD 250**.

## The deferral rule from February 2027

For exams from **February 2027**, the paid deferral option ends. If you miss your exam you forfeit the fee and register again, at the standard rate, for a later window. Emergency deferrals still exist for genuine emergencies, but pregnancy alone no longer qualifies (CFA Institute offers accommodations instead). CFA Institute's stated reason: candidates who defer pass at much lower rates than first-time takers (for example 29% against 50% at Level I in August 2025).

## Before you book

The window dates and deadlines above come from prep-provider calendars that reproduce CFA Institute's schedule. The fees and the deferral rule come from CFA Institute's own announcements. Always confirm on CFA Institute's dates and fees page before paying.

## Questions people ask

### How much does a CFA exam cost?

The fee depends on the level and on how early you register. Early registration is meaningfully cheaper. See the table above for current amounts and confirm them on the official site.

### Is there still a one-time enrolment fee?

No. The one-time program enrolment fee was abolished in 2025.

### What happens if I miss the deadline?

Registration closes for that window. You can register for a later window instead, usually at a new early or standard fee.

### Can I reschedule my exam?

Rescheduling within the same window is usually possible for a fee, subject to seat availability. Check the current policy before your appointment.
