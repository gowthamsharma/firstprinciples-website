## How to register for a CFA exam

1. **Check you are eligible.** For Level I you need a bachelor's degree, to be within 23 months of graduating, or 4,000 hours of work or higher education, plus a valid international passport. See [CFA requirements](blog-cfa-requirements.html).
2. **Create a CFA Institute account** with your name exactly as it appears on your passport.
3. **Choose the level and window**, and pay the early or standard fee before the deadline.
4. **Schedule your appointment** (date, time and test centre) before the scheduling deadline. Popular centres fill early.
5. **Keep the rescheduling deadline in mind.** You can move within the window for USD 250 until that date.

## How often you can sit the exams

You can take CFA exams **at most twice in a calendar year**, and **not in consecutive windows or in windows within six months of each other**. That is why, for example, a candidate who sits Level I in February cannot sit Level II in May of the same year, but can sit it in August or November.

Each level can be attempted at most six times in total.

## The deferral rule from February 2027

For exams from **February 2027**, the paid deferral option ends. If you miss your exam you forfeit the fee and register again, at the standard rate, for a later window. Emergency deferrals still exist for genuine emergencies, but pregnancy alone no longer qualifies (CFA Institute offers accommodations instead). CFA Institute's stated reason: candidates who defer pass at much lower rates than first-time takers (for example 29% against 50% at Level I in August 2025).

## When the 2027 results come out

Results arrive five to nine weeks after each window closes: Level I first, Level II about a week later, Level III last. Expected dates for every 2027 window are on our [CFA results dates](blog-cfa-results-dates.html) page.

## Questions people ask

### When is the CFA Level 1 exam in 2027?

In four windows: 22–28 February, 11–17 May, 15–22 August and 13–19 November 2027. February registration has closed; May is open until 10 February 2027 and August until 29 April 2027.

### When is the CFA Level 2 exam in 2027?

In three windows: 18–22 May, 23–27 August and 20–24 November 2027. There is no February Level II window.

### When is the CFA Level 3 exam in 2027?

Twice: 18–21 February and 11–14 August 2027. February registration closed on 5 November 2026; August registration is open until 29 April 2027, with the early fee until 20 January 2027.

### What is the last date to register for the CFA exam?

It depends on the window. For May 2027, registration closes on 10 February 2027; for August 2027, on 29 April 2027. Deadlines close at 11:59 pm US Eastern Time.

### How much is the CFA Level 1 exam fee in 2027?

USD 1,140 if you register by the early deadline and USD 1,490 after it. There is no longer a separate enrolment fee.

### Can I take Level I and Level II in the same year?

Yes, if the two windows are at least six months apart and not consecutive: for example, Level I in February and Level II in August or November. You must also have passed Level I before Level II, so the second window needs to be after your results arrive.

### When will the November 2027 registration open?

CFA Institute has not published it yet. Based on recent years, registration for a November window usually opens in the February before it.
