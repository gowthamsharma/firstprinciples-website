Level III is about **synthesis**: given a client, a market view and constraints, what portfolio would you build, how would you manage its risk, and how would you judge whether it worked? It is the only level with written answers.

## Format

- **11 item sets and 11 constructed-response (essay) sets**, each worth **12 points**.
- Two of the 22 sets (one of each kind) are trial questions that do not count; you will not know which.
- Item sets have **4 multiple-choice questions** worth 3 points each.
- **4 hours 24 minutes**, in two sessions of **132 minutes**, each mixing item sets and essay sets (6 and 5, or 5 and 6).
- Exam windows: **February and August** only.

## Core topics and your pathway

About two thirds of the exam is a **core** that every candidate shares; the remaining 30–35% is the **pathway** you choose when you register.

[[fig:weights_l3|Level III topic weight ranges: five core topics plus the pathway.]]

| Topic area | Share of exam |
|---|---|
| Your chosen pathway | 30–35% |
| Asset allocation | 15–20% |
| Building portfolios | 15–20% |
| Derivatives and risk | 10–15% |
| Ethics | 10–15% |
| Measuring performance | 5–10% |

The three pathways are:

- **Portfolio Management:** index and active equity strategies, fixed-income strategies, trade execution, and an integrated case study.
- **Private Markets:** private versus public markets, private equity, private debt, special situations, and private real estate and infrastructure.
- **Private Wealth:** wealth-management business models, client profiling, goals-based planning, investment planning, risk mitigation and wealth transfer.

All three pathways lead to the same charter. How to choose: [Choosing a Level III pathway](blog-level-3-pathways.html).

## What this means for how you study

- **Writing is a skill to practise, not an afterthought.** Graders award points for correct, specific answers that follow the command word, not for length or style. See [Answering Level III constructed-response questions](blog-level-3-constructed-response.html).
- **Use the official mocks.** From 2026, CFA Institute's Level III mock exams include scoring rubrics, so you can mark your own written answers.
- **Recent pass rates:** 50% (February 2026) and 47% (August 2026). First-time candidates did better: 59% and 56%.

## Questions people ask

### What is the format of CFA Level 3?

A mix of multiple-choice item sets and written-answer sets, taken in two sessions. Part of the exam comes from the pathway you choose.

### How much does the pathway count?

About a third of the exam. The rest is a core covering asset allocation, portfolio construction, performance measurement, derivatives and risk management, and ethics.

### When is Level 3 offered?

In two windows a year. Check the current calendar for exact dates and deadlines.

### Is Level 3 harder than Level 2?

It is different: less calculation, more judgement and written explanation. Many candidates find the written answers the hardest part to practise.
