Level III is about **synthesis**: given a client, a market view and constraints, what portfolio would you build, how would you manage its risk, and how would you judge whether it worked? It is the only level with written answers.

## Format

- **11 item sets and 11 constructed-response (essay) sets**, each worth **12 points**.
- Two of the 22 sets (one of each kind) are trial questions that do not count; you will not know which.
- Item sets have **4 multiple-choice questions** worth 3 points each.
- **4 hours 24 minutes**, in two sessions of **132 minutes**, each mixing item sets and essay sets (6 and 5, or 5 and 6).
- Exam windows: **February and August** only.

## Core topics and your pathway

About two thirds of the exam is a **core** that every candidate shares; the remaining 30–35% is the **pathway** you choose when you register.

[[fig:weights_l3|Level III topic weight ranges: five core topics plus the pathway.]]

| Topic area | Share of exam |
|---|---|
| Your chosen pathway | 30–35% |
| Asset allocation | 15–20% |
| Building portfolios | 15–20% |
| Derivatives and risk | 10–15% |
| Ethics | 10–15% |
| Measuring performance | 5–10% |

The three pathways are:

- **Portfolio Management:** index and active equity strategies, fixed-income strategies, trade execution, and an integrated case study.
- **Private Markets:** private versus public markets, private equity, private debt, special situations, and private real estate and infrastructure.
- **Private Wealth:** wealth-management business models, client profiling, goals-based planning, investment planning, risk mitigation and wealth transfer.

All three pathways lead to the same charter. How to choose: [Choosing a Level III pathway](blog-level-3-pathways.html).

## What this means for how you study

- **Writing is a skill to practise, not an afterthought.** Graders award points for correct, specific answers that follow the command word, not for length or style. See [Answering Level III constructed-response questions](blog-level-3-constructed-response.html).
- **Use the official mocks.** From 2026, CFA Institute's Level III mock exams include scoring rubrics, so you can mark your own written answers.
- **Recent pass rates:** 50% (February 2026) and 47% (August 2026). First-time candidates did better: 59% and 56%.


## How the core topics connect

Level III is easiest to understand as one process for managing a client's money. Each core topic is a step in it:

1. **Understand the client:** objectives, constraints and the investment policy statement.
2. **Form capital market expectations:** what returns and risks each asset class is likely to deliver.
3. **Set the strategic asset allocation**, for an institution, an individual or a liability.
4. **Build the portfolio:** active or passive, which managers, which strategies.
5. **Manage risk with derivatives:** change duration or equity exposure, hedge currencies, protect against falls.
6. **Measure the result:** performance attribution, appraisal ratios, manager selection.
7. **Ethics** throughout, including the Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS).

Exam cases usually follow the same sequence, so learning the topics in this order makes the questions feel familiar.

## What a constructed-response question looks like

A case describes a client or a portfolio in a page or so. Two to four questions follow, each worth a few points. A typical question gives a command word and a scope, for example: *"Determine which of the three portfolios is most appropriate for the client. Justify your response."* The answer box is small on purpose: graders want the decision and one or two specific reasons, not an essay.

Each set is worth 12 points and each session lasts 132 minutes with 11 sets, so you have roughly **one minute per point**. Use it as a pacing rule: a 4-point question deserves about four minutes.

## A study plan for Level III

Most candidates spend **300 hours or more** on Level III, but the mix is different from earlier levels:

| Share of time | Activity |
|---|---|
| About 50% | Learning the core and pathway readings |
| About 25% | Writing constructed-response answers and marking them against rubrics |
| About 25% | Item sets, mock exams and review |

Start writing answers by your fourth week. Candidates who leave writing until the last month often know the material but cannot express it in the time allowed.

## Questions people ask

### What is the format of CFA Level 3?

A mix of multiple-choice item sets and written-answer sets, taken in two sessions. Part of the exam comes from the pathway you choose.

### How much does the pathway count?

About a third of the exam. The rest is a core covering asset allocation, portfolio construction, performance measurement, derivatives and risk management, and ethics.

### When is Level 3 offered?

In two windows a year. Check the current calendar for exact dates and deadlines.

### Is Level 3 harder than Level 2?

It is different: less calculation, more judgement and written explanation. Many candidates find the written answers the hardest part to practise.


### How many hours should I study for Level 3?

Plan for 300 hours or more, with a quarter of that time spent writing practice answers. Writing under time pressure is the skill most candidates underestimate.

### Can I answer Level 3 essay questions in bullet points?

Yes. Graders mark the content, not the style. Short phrases or bullet points that answer exactly what was asked score as well as full sentences and take less time.

### When is CFA Level 3 in 2027, and what is the registration deadline?

Level III runs on 18–21 February and 11–14 August 2027. February registration closed on 5 November 2026. For August 2027, the early fee applies until 20 January 2027 and registration closes on 29 April 2027. See [CFA exam dates 2027](cfa-dates-fees.html).
