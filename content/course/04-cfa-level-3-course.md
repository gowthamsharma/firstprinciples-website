Level III is about **synthesis**: given a client, a market view and constraints, what portfolio would you build, and how would you judge whether it worked? Half the exam is written, so the course trains you to answer in short, exact sentences, and it explains each strategy from the trade-off it manages rather than as a list to remember.

[[figure]]

## What the course covers

| Area | Share of exam | What the lessons focus on |
|---|---|---|
| Your pathway | 30–35% | Portfolio Management first: index-based and active equity, fixed-income strategies, trade execution |
| Asset allocation | 15–20% | Capital market expectations, asset-only and liability-relative allocation, real-world constraints |
| Portfolio construction | 15–20% | Equity and fixed income portfolio building, alternatives, private wealth and institutional clients |
| Derivatives and risk | 10–15% | Hedging with options, swaps, forwards and futures; currency management |
| Ethics | 10–15% | The Standards applied to portfolio managers, and GIPS |
| Performance measurement | 5–10% | Attribution, appraisal measures, manager selection |

## The first lessons in production

The first Level III lessons cover the Portfolio Management pathway's equity readings: **index-based equity strategies** (how index funds track, what drives tracking error, securities lending) and **active equity strategies** (fundamental and quantitative approaches, factor investing, activist strategies). They are animated from first principles, with Level III-style written questions at the end.

## Written answers, practised properly

Constructed-response questions are marked against a short list of points. The course shows you the points the marking looks for, how to state each one in a sentence, and which command words (justify, discuss, calculate) change what is expected. See [how to write Level III answers](blog-level-3-constructed-response.html).

## Questions people ask

### Which Level III pathway does the course cover?

Portfolio Management first, because it is the pathway most candidates choose and the one closest to the core. Private Wealth and Private Markets may follow.

### When are the Level III exams?

Level III runs in two windows a year, February and August. The February 2027 window is 18 to 21 February.
