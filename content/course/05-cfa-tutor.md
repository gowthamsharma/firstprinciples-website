Not passing a CFA exam is common: in most recent Level I windows more than half of candidates failed. What matters is what you change before the next attempt. This **30-minute 1-to-1 session** is for candidates who sat Level I, II or III and did not pass. You bring your score report; you leave with a clear picture of where the marks went and a plan you can follow up to your next exam window.

## Who the session is for

- You sat a CFA exam (Level I, II or III) and **did not pass**, in any window.
- You plan to sit the same level again and want an outside view before you start.
- You can share your score report, or at least your result band and the topic bars.

If you haven't sat an exam yet, the [study guides](how-to-prepare.html) and the [study planner](free-resources.html#planner) are a better place to start.

## What happens in 30 minutes

| Time | What we do |
|---|---|
| Before | You answer three short questions when you book: your level, the window you sat, and how you prepared (hours, materials, mocks). Send your score report by email. |
| 0–10 min | **Read your score report together.** How far you were from the minimum passing score, and which weak topics cost the most marks given their exam weight. |
| 10–22 min | **Diagnose the method, not just the effort.** Question practice, mock timing, how you handled item sets or constructed response, and what to stop doing. |
| 22–30 min | **Build the retake plan:** the window to aim for, weekly hours, topic order, and the dates for your mocks. Your questions. |
| After | A short written summary of the plan by email within 48 hours. |

The session runs on a video call. You need a camera only if you want one; a working microphone is enough.

## What the session is not

- **No exam questions.** Nobody may share actual CFA exam questions, and the session never uses them. It also does not use CFA Institute curriculum material.
- **No guarantee of a pass.** The plan works only if you follow it; most of the work happens in the weeks after the call.
- **Not a full course.** Thirty minutes is for diagnosis and planning. We can work through one or two concepts that tripped you up, but not re-teach a topic.

## Questions people ask

### How much does a CFA tutor cost?

Here, a 30-minute 1-to-1 session costs €25. Because it focuses on your score report and your retake plan rather than re-teaching topics, one session is often enough; book a second one closer to the exam if you want a check on your mock results.

### Do you tutor for CFA Level II and Level III as well as Level I?

Yes. The session works for retakers at every level. For Level II it often focuses on item-set technique and time per vignette; for Level III on constructed-response answers and how marks are lost through wording.

### I failed CFA Level 1. What should I do first?

Take a week off, then read the score report properly and check when you can sit again under the attempt rules. Our step-by-step [guide for candidates who failed Level I](blog-failed-cfa-level-1.html) covers this, with a 20-week retake plan. Book a session if you want a second pair of eyes on your report.

### When can I retake the CFA exam?

You may sit each level up to six times, at most twice in a calendar year, and not in consecutive windows or windows less than six months apart. The [CFA exam dates 2027](cfa-dates-fees.html) page lists every window and registration deadline.
