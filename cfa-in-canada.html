CFA in Canada: exam centres, fees in CAD, salary and recognition

By Gowtham KumarFacts checked 22 Nov 2026

Where to sit the CFA exams in Canada, what they cost in CAD, what people with the CFA earn there, which employers hire them and how local regulators treat the charter. Checked 22 November 2026.

Canada has one of the largest charterholder communities in the world, and its banks and pension funds are among the largest employers of charterholders in the world. The charter also has a formal place in Canadian securities rules: it is one of the main routes to registering as a portfolio manager.

Where to take the CFA exams in Canada

The exams are computer-based and run at Prometric test centres. CFA Institute's planned list names 15 cities: Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Ottawa, Winnipeg, Halifax, Hamilton, London (Ontario), Quebec City, Regina, Saskatoon, St. John's and Victoria. You choose a centre and a time when you schedule, after registering; popular centres fill up, so schedule as soon as your window opens. You need a valid international passport, and the name on it must match your CFA account exactly.

What the CFA costs in CAD

CFA Institute charges the same US-dollar fees everywhere. Local amounts use European Central Bank reference rates of 20 November 2026 (1 USD = 1.4307 CAD) and are rounded.

Exam Early (USD) Early (CAD) Standard (USD) Standard (CAD)
Level I USD 1,140 C$1,630 USD 1,490 C$2,130
Level II USD 1,140 C$1,630 USD 1,490 C$2,130
Level III USD 1,240 C$1,770 USD 1,590 C$2,270
All three levels USD 3,520 C$5,040 USD 4,570 C$6,540
  • There is no enrolment fee any more; it was abolished in April 2025.
  • Rescheduling within your window costs USD 250. From February 2027 the paid deferral option ends: if you miss an exam you lose the fee.
  • Study materials come on top: CFA Institute's own curriculum is included with registration, while third-party courses cost anywhere from a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per level.
  • At early registration, the three exams together cost about 3 weeks of the average base salary reported by people with the CFA in Canada.

See 2027 exam dates and fees for every window and deadline, and the total cost of the CFA for materials and retakes.

What people with the CFA earn in Canada

The average base salary reported to PayScale by people who list the CFA in Canada is C$89,000 a year (about US$62,000), based on 328 profiles, last updated April 2026. That group mixes charterholders with candidates and juniors with seniors, so treat it as a typical figure, not a charterholder's ceiling. Bonuses come on top.

Compare it with CFA salaries in 19 countries and pay by job.

Who hires charterholders in Canada

  • RBC: 2,532 charterholders
  • TD: 1,481 charterholders
  • BMO Financial Group: 1,213 charterholders
  • CIBC: 1,071 charterholders
  • Scotiabank: 1,028 charterholders
  • Manulife: 626 charterholders
  • Sun Life: 515 charterholders
  • National Bank of Canada: 508 charterholders
  • Desjardins: 355 charterholders

RBC employs more charterholders than any company in the world except JPMorgan Chase, and all five big Canadian banks are in the global top 16. Pension managers such as CPP Investments, Ontario Teachers' and the Caisse in Quebec are major employers too.

CFA Institute's employer figures are self-reported by charterholders and count people worldwide, not only in Canada.

How the charter is recognised in Canada

  • Portfolio manager registration. Under the national registration rules, an advising representative (portfolio management) qualifies with the CFA charter plus 12 months of relevant investment management experience in the previous 36 months. An associate advising representative qualifies with Level I passed plus 24 months of experience. The Canadian Investment Manager (CIM) designation is the main alternative route.
  • Quebec tax credit. CFA Institute is a recognised educational institution for Revenu Québec, so Quebec residents may be able to claim a tax credit for exam fees.
  • Provincial rules differ in detail. Check with your provincial securities commission before relying on a route.

Rules change. Before you rely on any exemption, confirm it with the regulator or your employer's compliance team.

The local CFA society

CFA Societies Canada brings together the local societies, including CFA Society Toronto (one of the largest in the world), CFA Society Vancouver, CFA Montréal, CFA Society Calgary, CFA Society Edmonton, CFA Society Ottawa and others. Membership is open to charterholders and, as affiliate or candidate members in many societies, to people still working through the exams.

Fees in Canadian dollars

CFA Institute charges in US dollars, so the Canadian-dollar cost moves with the exchange rate. Your card issuer will also add a foreign-currency fee unless your card has none.

Scholarships

CFA Institute's Access Scholarship reduces the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates with financial need. It opens four times a year for about three weeks. The window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027 and closes on 2 March 2027. Universities that are CFA Institute affiliates also receive student scholarships to distribute.

Questions people ask

How much does the CFA cost in Canada?

At early registration Level I costs USD 1,140, about C$1,630, and all three levels USD 3,520, about C$5,040 at November 2026 exchange rates. Standard registration costs more: USD 4,570 in total. Study materials are extra.

Do I need the CFA to be a portfolio manager in Canada?

Not strictly: the CIM designation with more experience is the other main route. But the charter plus 12 months of relevant experience is the most common way to register as an advising representative.

Which Canadian employers hire the most charterholders?

RBC, TD, BMO, CIBC and Scotiabank, then insurers such as Manulife and Sun Life, according to CFA Institute's 2025 employer list.

Can I get a scholarship for the CFA exams?

Yes. CFA Institute's Access Scholarship cuts the exam fee to USD 400 for candidates who need financial help. Applications open four times a year; the window for the November 2027 exams opens on 9 February 2027.
