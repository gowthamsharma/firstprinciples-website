How much does the CFA cost in total?

Exam fees alone come to USD 3,520 to 4,570 for all three levels. Add study materials and any retakes, and most candidates spend somewhere between USD 4,000 and 8,000.

2 min read  /  By Gowtham Kumar

The cost of the CFA Program has three parts: exam fees, which are fixed; study materials, which are your choice; and retakes, which you hope to avoid. Here is how they add up.

Exam fees

Since 2025 there is no one-time enrolment fee; you pay per exam. From February 2026:

Level Early registration Standard registration
Level I USD 1,140 USD 1,490
Level II USD 1,140 USD 1,490
Level III USD 1,240 USD 1,590
All three USD 3,520 USD 4,570

Registering early saves USD 350 per level, more than USD 1,000 over the program. Rescheduling inside a window costs USD 250.

Study materials

The exam fee includes CFA Institute's own curriculum, practice questions and mock exams. Many candidates add third-party materials:

  • question banks and mock packs: usually a few hundred dollars per level;
  • full video courses with notes: roughly USD 500 to 1,500 per level, depending on the provider and package.

You do not need everything. Buy for the gap you have (see choosing prep materials).

Retakes

With fewer than half of candidates passing each level, budgeting for one retake is realistic. A retake costs a full exam fee again, plus months of time.

A realistic total

Scenario Rough total (USD)
Curriculum only, no retakes, early registration about 3,500
Question bank each level, one retake about 5,500
Full prep course each level, one retake about 7,500–8,500

Material prices vary by provider and change often; check them before you budget.

Ways to pay less

  • Register early for every exam.
  • Scholarships. CFA Institute offers an Access Scholarship for people who cannot afford the fees, a Student Scholarship through affiliated universities, and a Professor Scholarship.
  • Employer sponsorship. Many investment firms pay exam fees and give study leave; ask before you register.
  • Avoid retakes with steady preparation; the cheapest exam is the one you pass first time.

Questions people ask

Is there still a CFA enrolment fee?

No. The one-time program enrolment fee was abolished in 2025. You pay only per exam.

How much is CFA Level 1?

USD 1,140 with early registration or USD 1,490 at the standard fee, from February 2026.

Are there CFA scholarships?

Yes: the Access, Student and Professor scholarships. Each has its own eligibility rules and deadlines on the CFA Institute website.

Is the CFA cheaper than an MBA?

Usually, by a wide margin. All three exam fees together are a few thousand dollars, while MBA tuition at well-known schools commonly runs to tens of thousands or more.

How long does the CFA take?

The fastest possible route through all three exams is about a year, and 18 months is a more realistic fast track; most candidates take two to four years. Then you need 4,000 hours of work experience for the charter.

CFA exam results: when they arrive and how to read them

Results come by email five to nine weeks after your exam window closes, as a simple pass or fail plus a score report. The pass mark is never published, so the report is about where you stand, not a number to hit.
