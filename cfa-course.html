The First Principles CFA course: Levels I, II and III, taught from the ground up

By Gowtham KumarThe course

A CFA course built on understanding rather than memorising: short animated video lessons for every topic, the intuition before the formula, and exam-style questions solved step by step. What it covers, how it works, what is free today and when it opens.

Most CFA courses teach you what to remember. This one teaches you why each result has to be true, so that you can rebuild it under exam pressure, apply it to an unfamiliar vignette and explain it in a Level III written answer. That is what the exams increasingly reward, and it is what "first principles" means.

What the course is

  • Video lessons for every topic at all three levels, typically 8 to 15 minutes each, built with animated diagrams rather than slides of bullet points.
  • The intuition first. Every lesson starts from a question a practitioner would ask, reaches the idea with a picture or an everyday analogy, and only then names the formula.
  • Pause and predict. Short questions inside each lesson ask you to commit to an answer before you see it, which is what makes ideas stick.
  • Exam questions, solved. Each topic ends with questions in the exam's format, answered the way the exam marks them, including why each wrong option is wrong.
  • Written by a practitioner. The course is built by Gowtham Kumar, who works in fixed income index management in Frankfurt and holds an MSc in Quantitative Finance.

What you can use today, free

  • The prerequisites course: 50 graded lessons in financial statement analysis and economics, with your progress saved in the browser.
  • Level I practice questions: 100 original questions across all ten topics, with worked answers.
  • A BA II Plus in your browser: every exam calculation, played key by key.
  • The study planner: an Excel workbook that builds your week-by-week plan and keeps your streak.
  • Are you ready for Level I?: a five-minute self-check before you pay the fee.

How it differs from a typical prep course

Large prep providers are good at coverage: every reading summarised, a big question bank, mock exams. Their weakness is the one candidates complain about most: thousands of facts to memorise, with little sense of how they connect. This course does less summarising and more explaining. It is designed to sit alongside CFA Institute's own curriculum and practice questions (included with your exam registration), not to replace them.

Who it is for

  • Career changers and non-finance graduates (engineers, scientists, lawyers) who want the foundations explained properly. See the CFA from any background.
  • Candidates who failed once and know that more of the same memorising will not work. See what to do after failing.
  • Level II and III candidates who need to apply ideas to cases rather than recall them.

Questions people ask

Is First Principles an approved CFA prep provider?

Not yet. CFA Institute runs a Prep Provider Guidelines program; First Principles is independent, and CFA Institute does not endorse, promote or warrant its products. All explanations are original and are not taken from the CFA Program curriculum.

Can I pass the CFA with this course alone?

Use it with CFA Institute's curriculum, practice questions and mock exams, which come with your registration. The course explains the ideas; the official material defines exactly what is examined.

Which level is available first?

Free material for Level I (practice questions, the prerequisites course, the calculator guide) is available now. The first video lessons in production are for Level III's Portfolio Management pathway and for Level I.

Do I need a finance background?

No. The prerequisites course starts from the basics of accounting and economics, and every lesson builds from ideas you already know.
