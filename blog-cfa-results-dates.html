CFA results dates for Level I, II and III: every window in 2026 and 2027

By Gowtham KumarStudy blog

When CFA results come out, level by level: the actual release dates for every 2026 exam window, the expected dates for the November 2026 and 2027 windows, and how many days after the exam each level usually takes.

CFA Institute emails results five to nine weeks after an exam window closes. Within that range, the order is always the same: Level I first, Level II a few days to a week later, and Level III last, because its written answers are graded by hand. This page lists the actual dates for every 2026 window and what to expect next. For what the result email and score report contain, see our guide to CFA exam results.

Results dates in 2026

Exam window Level I results Level II results Level III results
November 2025 13 January 2026 (43% passed) 15 January 2026 (42% passed) — (no November window)
February 2026 19 March 2026 (45% passed) — (no February window) 7 April 2026 (50% passed)
May 2026 23 June 2026 (39% passed) 25 June 2026 (43% passed) — (no May window)
August 2026 24 September 2026 (43% passed) 30 September 2026 (45% passed) 20 October 2026 (47% passed)

Dates are when CFA Institute released results and published the pass rates.

What to expect next

Exam window Level I Level II Level III
November 2026 expected mid-January 2027 expected mid-January 2027 —

These are estimates based on the pattern of the last two years. CFA Institute confirms each date by email and on its exam results page a few weeks in advance; trust that over any estimate, including ours.

When do CFA Level I results come out?

About five weeks after the Level I window closes. In 2026, Level I results arrived on 19 March (February window), 23 June (May), 24 September (August), and on 13 January for the November 2025 window, which ran over the holiday period. Level I is all multiple choice, so it is always released first.

When do CFA Level II results come out?

About six weeks after the window, a few days to a week after Level I. In 2026: 15 January (November 2025 window), 25 June (May) and 30 September (August). Level II is also all multiple choice, but its results follow Level I in each window.

When do CFA Level III results come out?

About seven to nine weeks after the window, the last of the three. In 2026: 7 April (February window) and 20 October (August). The extra time is for grading the constructed-response (written) answers, which are marked by charterholder graders against a guideline.

How many days after the exam do CFA results come out?

Counting from the end of the window:

Level Typical wait after the window closes Roughly, in days
Level I about 5 weeks 30–40 days
Level II about 5–6 weeks 35–45 days
Level III about 7–9 weeks 50–65 days

If you sat early in a window, your wait is longer than this, because results are released for everyone at once after the window closes and the minimum passing score has been set.

Why the dates matter for your next registration

  • Passed Level I in February? Results in March leave time to register for Level II in August, but the study time is short. Most candidates choose November.
  • Passed Level II in May or August? Level III is offered only in February and August, so the next realistic window is usually February or August of the following year.
  • Did not pass? Check the retake rules: at most two attempts at a level in a year, with at least six months between them. Our guide to what to do after failing Level I explains the options.

Registration deadlines for each window are on our dates and fees page.

Questions people ask

When will the November 2026 CFA results be released?

Expect Level I and Level II results in mid-January 2027. In the previous November window, results came on 13 January (Level I) and 15 January (Level II). CFA Institute will confirm the exact date by email.

Do you get CFA results immediately after the exam?

No. You receive only a confirmation that you completed the exam. Results are released for all candidates at once, five to nine weeks after the window closes.

Why do Level III results take longer?

About half of Level III is written answers, which graders mark by hand against a guideline. That takes several more weeks than scoring multiple-choice exams.

At what time are CFA results released?

CFA Institute sends results by email and posts them in your account on the release date. The time varies, so check both your inbox (and spam folder) and your CFA Institute account.

Where can I see the CFA pass rates for each window?

Our pass rates page lists every window by level, with five- and ten-year averages.
