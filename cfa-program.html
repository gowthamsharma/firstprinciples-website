CFA Program guide

The CFA Program in one page

What the CFA Program is, how the three levels fit together, what it costs, how long it takes and what you need for the charter, using CFA Institute's published facts.

4 min read  /  By Gowtham Kumar

The CFA® Program is a three-exam, self-study qualification run by CFA Institute. Each level builds on the one before: Level I teaches the tools, Level II applies them to valuation, and Level III uses everything to manage a portfolio for a real client. Passing all three is necessary for the charter, but it is not sufficient on its own (see the last section).

The three levels at a glance

Level I Level II Level III
Focus Tools and concepts Valuation and analysis Portfolio management and wealth planning
Format 180 multiple-choice questions 22 item sets (88 questions) 11 item sets + 11 constructed-response sets
Time 2 sessions of about 2 h 15 min 4 h 24 min in 2 sessions 4 h 24 min in 2 sessions
2027 windows Feb, May, Aug, Nov May, Aug, Nov Feb, Aug
Latest pass rate 43% (Aug 2026) 45% (Aug 2026) 47% (Aug 2026)

Each level has its own page: Level I, Level II and Level III.

How long it takes

Candidates who pass usually put in 300-plus hours per level. Spread over five or six months, that is roughly 12 to 15 hours a week. Most people take two to four years to finish all three levels, because there is a gap between results and the next sitting, and because Level III is only offered twice a year.

What it costs

Since 29 April 2025 there is no one-time enrolment fee (it used to be USD 350). For exams sat from February 2026 onwards, CFA Institute's registration fees are:

Early registration Standard registration
Level I USD 1,140 USD 1,490
Level II USD 1,140 USD 1,490
Level III USD 1,240 USD 1,590

Registering early saves USD 350 at every level. The full timetable with deadlines is on exam dates and fees.

A rule that changes in 2027

From the February 2027 exam cycle, CFA Institute is ending the paid deferral option. If you do not sit your booked exam, you forfeit the registration fee and must register again, at the standard rate, for a later window. Choose your window carefully.

Practical Skills Modules

At each level you must complete one Practical Skills Module (for example, Python programming fundamentals) before your result is released. A module counts for one level only, so you choose a different one each time.

Beyond the exams: the charter

To use the CFA designation you need to:

  • pass Levels I, II and III;
  • build up 4,000 hours of qualifying investment work, spread across 36 months or more (it can come before, during or after the exams, and part-time work counts);
  • supply references from people who know your work, and join the institute as a member.

Until then, the correct wording on a CV is that you are a Level II or Level III candidate in the CFA Program, or that you have passed a level. "CFA" is never a noun or a degree you hold before the charter is awarded.

Questions people ask

How many years does the CFA charter take?

Most candidates take two to four years to pass all three levels, then complete the work experience requirement. Experience gained before or during the exams counts, so many finish soon after Level III.

Do I need a finance degree?

No. You need a bachelor's degree, to be close to finishing one, or enough combined study and work experience; the exact cut-offs are in the entry rules. Candidates come from engineering, science and many other fields.

Is the CFA charter worth it?

It is widely respected in investment management, research and wealth management, and the knowledge is broad and practical. It is a large time commitment, so it pays most when your career is in investments.

Can I take the levels in any order?

No. Each level must be passed before the next one can be taken.
