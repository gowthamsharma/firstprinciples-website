CFA requirements: degree, passport, work experience and who can register

By Gowtham KumarStudy blog

You do not need a finance degree, or even a finished degree, to start. You do need a passport, English, and either a degree in sight or enough work experience. Here is exactly when each level opens up.

The rules for starting the CFA Program are looser than most people expect, and the rules for finishing it are stricter. It helps to keep the two apart: registering for the exams and earning the charter are different gates.

Who can register for Level I

You qualify for the exams through one of three routes:

  • A finished bachelor's degree (or an equivalent programme), in any subject.
  • Still studying. Undergraduates can register as long as their Level I window falls within 23 months of their graduation month. Level II then requires you to be within 11 months of graduating, and Level III requires the degree to be finished.
  • Work and education combined. No degree at all? You qualify with 4,000 hours of professional work, higher education, or a mix of both, built up over at least three consecutive years by the time you register.

There is no minimum grade, no entrance test, and no requirement to have studied finance. Engineers, scientists and lawyers sit the exams every year.

The practical requirements people forget

  • A valid international travel passport is the only accepted ID, both to register and on exam day. The name in your CFA account must match it exactly. No passport, no exam.
  • English. The exams are offered only in English.
  • A Practical Skills Module at each level. These short online modules do not affect your score, but you will not receive your result until you finish one.
  • An approved calculator: the Texas Instruments BA II Plus family or the HP 12C family.

Limits on attempts

You can take each level at most six times, and at most twice in a calendar year, with at least six months between attempts at the same level. In practice that means a failed attempt costs you at least half a year.

Requirements for the charter itself

Passing Level III makes you a candidate who has passed, not a charterholder. To use the letters "CFA" after your name you must also:

  1. Pass all three levels, in order.
  2. Build 4,000 hours of relevant work experience over at least 36 months. It can come before, during or after the exams, and part-time work counts. The work must be part of, or feed into, investment decision-making.
  3. Apply for membership of CFA Institute, with two or three professional references, and join a local society if you wish.

Planning around the rules

  • If you are a student, the 23-month rule lets you pass Level I before you graduate, which helps on a CV.
  • Renew your passport early if it might expire near exam day.
  • Track your work hours as you go; reconstructing three years of experience later is painful.

Questions people ask

Can I do the CFA without a degree?

Yes. If you do not have a bachelor's degree, 4,000 hours of combined work and higher education over at least three consecutive years qualifies you for the exams.

Can I do the CFA without a finance or maths background?

Yes. The curriculum starts from the basics, including statistics and accounting. A non-finance background means more study hours at Level I, not a barrier to entry.

Can I register without a passport?

No. A valid international travel passport is required to register and to sit the exam. Apply for one before you register.

Do I need work experience before I start?

No. Experience is needed for the charter, not for the exams, and you can build it while you study.

How much does the CFA cost in total?

Exam fees alone come to USD 3,520 to 4,570 for all three levels. Add study materials and any retakes, and most candidates spend somewhere between USD 4,000 and 8,000.

Study strategyBy Gowtham KumarStudy blog3 min read

How long does the CFA take?

The fastest possible route through all three exams is about a year, and 18 months is a more realistic fast track; most candidates take two to four years. Then you need 4,000 hours of work experience for the charter.
