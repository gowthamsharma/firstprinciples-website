CFA salary and jobs: what the charter is worth to your pay

The charter does not set your salary; your role, city and experience do. What it changes is which roles you can get, and how fast you move up in investment work.

3 min read  /  By Gowtham Kumar

"How much does a CFA earn?" is one of the most searched questions about the program, and it has no single answer. Pay in finance depends far more on the job you hold, the city you work in and your years of experience than on any letters after your name. What the charter changes is your access to certain jobs, and how credible you are when you get there.

What the data shows

Most salary figures online come from job sites or prep providers and mix many roles together. One of the clearest recent sources is CFA Institute's own 2026 compensation study for India, run by Kantar with about 2,770 respondents:

  • Charterholders awarded in 2024–2025, with about six years' experience on average, reported average income of about INR 32 lakh a year.
  • All charterholders, averaging about ten years' experience, reported about INR 66 lakh a year.
  • Over 70% of charterholders with eight or more years' experience earned more than INR 40 lakh.

The jump between the two groups is mostly experience. The study itself says pay keeps rising strongly with seniority after the charter. Treat any single "CFA salary" number with care; compare like with like.

Where charterholders work

The charter is designed for investment decision-making, so it carries most weight in:

  • equity and credit research;
  • portfolio and fund management;
  • wealth and private banking;
  • risk management and consulting;
  • index, product and investment strategy roles.

It helps less in roles where other credentials dominate: audit (CPA, ACCA, CA), personal financial planning in the US (CFP), or investment banking deal work, where experience and networks matter more than exams.

Does passing Level I or II raise pay?

Passing a level shows commitment and knowledge, and many employers note it on promotions or hiring. On its own it rarely triggers a raise. The value is cumulative: by Level III you can do work that Level I candidates cannot, and that is what employers pay for.

Making the charter pay

  • Pick roles where the charter is a signal, such as research, asset management and wealth management.
  • Use the curriculum at work. Valuation, portfolio construction and ethics are used daily in these jobs.
  • Combine it with technical skills (Python, data) or a sector specialism; the combination is rarer than either alone.

Questions people ask

What is the salary after CFA Level 3?

There is no fixed figure. Pay depends on role, city and experience. In CFA Institute's 2026 India study, recent charterholders with about six years' experience averaged around INR 32 lakh a year.

Do CFA charterholders earn more than non-charterholders?

In investment roles they often do, partly because the charter opens those roles and partly because charterholders tend to have more experience. Surveys rarely isolate the charter's effect from experience.

Is the CFA good for investment banking?

It helps with valuation and credibility, but banking recruitment leans on internships, networks and deal experience. Many bankers never take the exams.

How much does the CFA cost in total?

Exam fees alone come to USD 3,520 to 4,570 for all three levels. Add study materials and any retakes, and most candidates spend somewhere between USD 4,000 and 8,000.

Study strategyStudy blog3 min read

How long does the CFA take?

The fastest possible route through all three exams is about a year, and 18 months is a more realistic fast track; most candidates take two to four years. Then you need 4,000 hours of work experience for the charter.

Study strategyStudy blog3 min read

CFA exam results: when they arrive and how to read them

Results come by email five to nine weeks after your exam window closes, as a simple pass or fail plus a score report. The pass mark is never published, so the report is about where you stand, not a number to hit.
