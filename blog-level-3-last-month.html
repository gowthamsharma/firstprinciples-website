CFA Level III: the last four weeks before the February 2027 exam

By Gowtham KumarStudy blog

A week-by-week plan for the final month before the Level III window of 18 to 21 February 2027: mocks, written-answer drills, the pathway, ethics and GIPS, and the exam-day details that cost marks if you leave them late.

The February 2027 Level III window runs from 18 to 21 February. Four weeks out, the curriculum should be behind you. What separates a pass from a near miss now is mostly written-answer technique, time management and not leaving whole areas cold. This is how to spend the last month.

Where you should be four weeks out

  • Every core reading and your pathway covered at least once.
  • At least one full mock behind you, reviewed.
  • A short list of formulas and frameworks you still mix up.

If you are not there, do not panic and do not start the curriculum again. Spend the first week covering the uncovered readings at summary level with questions, and make the rest of the plan below shorter.

Week 4 (from about 21 January): full mock, then repair

  • Sit a full mock under exam conditions: two sessions of 132 minutes, essay and item sets mixed.
  • Review it for longer than it took to sit. For every written answer, compare your wording with the guideline answer: did you give the number of points asked for, and did each one justify itself?
  • Make a repair list of the five weakest areas and spend the rest of the week on them, with questions.

Week 3: written answers every day

Constructed-response sets are where most Level III marks are won and lost.

  • Do two essay sets a day, timed. The exam gives you about 12 minutes per 12-point set on average, so practise at that pace.
  • Answer the command word. "Justify" needs a reason; "discuss" needs both sides; "calculate" needs the working.
  • Write short, complete sentences, one point per line. Markers look for specific points; long paragraphs hide them.
  • Practise the formats the exam uses: recommend-and-justify tables, calculations with a one-line explanation, choosing between strategies.

See how to write Level III constructed responses for the marking logic.

Week 2: second mock, pathway and the small topics

  • Sit a second full mock early in the week and review it the same way.
  • Your pathway is 30–35% of the exam. If it has been a while, do a full pass of its question sets.
  • Ethics and GIPS are 10–15% and very predictable; do a set of ethics cases every day this week.
  • Performance measurement (5–10%) is short and formula-heavy: attribution, appraisal ratios, manager selection.

Week 1: light, sharp, rested

  • One half mock at most, early in the week. No new material after mid-week.
  • Re-read your error log and your list of frameworks (IPS constraints, risk tolerance factors, rebalancing ranges, hedge ratios).
  • Exam logistics: passport matching your CFA account name exactly, approved calculator with a spare battery, the route to the test centre, your appointment time in local time. See exam day.
  • Sleep. Tired candidates lose more marks to misreading than they gain from a last late night.

On the day

  • Bank the easy points. In essay sets, answer what you can quickly, then come back.
  • Do not leave blanks. A short, sensible answer earns partial credit; an empty box earns nothing.
  • Watch the clock per set, not per session: about 12 minutes per 12-point item set, a little more for essay sets that need calculations.

Questions people ask

When is the CFA Level III exam in February 2027?

The window runs from 18 to 21 February 2027. Your appointment date and time are on your confirmation.

How many mock exams should I take for Level III?

Two to four full mocks in the last five weeks, each followed by a thorough review, is enough for most candidates. Reviewing well matters more than the number.

When do February 2027 Level III results come out?

Results usually arrive five to nine weeks after the window closes, so expect them in late March or April 2027. CFA Institute emails the exact date in advance.
