Choosing a CFA Level III pathway

Portfolio Management, Private Markets or Private Wealth: all three lead to the same charter. Choose by the job you want and the material you already use, not by guesses about difficulty.

Study strategy · 2 min read

Since 2025, Level III candidates choose a specialised pathway worth 30–35% of the exam. The other 65–70% is a core shared by everyone: asset allocation, portfolio construction, performance measurement, derivatives and risk management, and ethics.

The three options

Pathway Covers Natural fit
Portfolio Management index and active equity, fixed-income strategies, trade execution, integrated case asset managers, institutional investors, index and ETF roles
Private Markets private equity, private debt, special situations, private real estate and infrastructure private capital, real assets, fund selection
Private Wealth client profiling, goals-based planning, investment planning, risk mitigation, wealth transfer private banks, advisers, family offices

How to choose

  1. The charter is identical. No pathway name appears on it, so the choice does not label you for life.
  2. Choose the material you will use. The pathway readings are some of the most job-relevant in the whole program. If you work with public-market portfolios, Portfolio Management reinforces your day job, and your day job reinforces your studying.
  3. Do not pick on rumoured difficulty. CFA Institute publishes one pass rate for Level III, not one per pathway, so claims that a pathway is easier are guesswork.
  4. Decide before you register. The choice is made at registration and is locked after a short cooling-off period, so treat it as binding for that sitting.
