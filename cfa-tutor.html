1-to-1 retake session: a CFA Level I and II tutor after a failed exam

By Gowtham KumarThe course

A 30-minute 1-to-1 video session for candidates who did not pass CFA Level I or Level II: we read your score report together, find where the marks went, and build a retake plan up to your next exam window. €25 per session.

Not passing a CFA exam is common: in most recent Level I windows more than half of candidates failed. What matters is what you change before the next attempt. The 1-to-1 retake session is a 30-minute video call for candidates who sat Level I or Level II and did not pass. You bring your score report; you leave with a clear picture of where the marks went and a plan you can follow up to your next exam window.

Choose a time30 minutes · €25 · Levels I and II · weekend slots up to six months ahead

Who the session is for

  • You sat CFA Level I or Level II and did not pass, in any window.
  • You plan to sit the same level again and want an outside view before you start.
  • You can share your score report, or at least your result band and the topic bars.

Sessions are not offered for Level III at the moment.

If you haven't sat an exam yet, the study guides and the study planner are a better place to start.

What happens in 30 minutes

Time What we do
Before You pick a time and fill in a short form: your level, the window you sat, your result band and how you prepared. You'll be asked to send your score report when the time is confirmed.
0–10 min Read your score report together. How far you were from the minimum passing score, and which weak topics cost the most marks given their exam weight.
10–22 min Diagnose the method, not just the effort. Question practice, mock timing, how you handled the questions (or the item sets at Level II), and what to stop doing.
22–30 min Build the retake plan: the window to aim for, weekly hours, topic order, and the dates for your mocks. Your questions.
After A short written summary of the plan by email within 48 hours.

The session runs on a video call. You need a camera only if you want one; a working microphone is enough.

Who you'll talk to

Gowtham Kumar writes every guide on this site. He works in fixed-income index management in Frankfurt, holds an MSc in Quantitative Finance from Frankfurt School of Finance & Management, has passed FRM Part I, and is a CFA Level III candidate. He has written the 100 practice questions, the formula sheets and the retake guide on this site.

Price and booking

  • €25 for 30 minutes. You pay nothing when you send the request. Once your time is confirmed, you get a payment link by email: pay by card (including Apple Pay and Google Pay) or with PayPal. Your card provider or PayPal converts it into your own currency.
  • Pick any open weekend time up to six months ahead. Times are shown in your own time zone, and a time is held for you as soon as you send the request.
  • Reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before the session for a full refund.

What the session is not

  • No exam questions. Nobody may share actual CFA exam questions, and the session never uses them. It also does not use CFA Institute curriculum material.
  • No guarantee of a pass. The plan works only if you follow it; most of the work happens in the weeks after the call.
  • Not a full course. Thirty minutes is for diagnosis and planning. We can work through one or two concepts that tripped you up, but not re-teach a topic.

Questions people ask

How much does a CFA tutor cost?

Here, a 30-minute 1-to-1 session costs €25. Because it focuses on your score report and your retake plan rather than re-teaching topics, one session is often enough; book a second one closer to the exam if you want a check on your mock results.

Do you tutor for CFA Level II as well as Level I?

Yes. Sessions are open to retakers at Level I and Level II. For Level II they often focus on item-set technique: reading the vignette for the inputs that matter and keeping to about 12 minutes per item set (11 item sets in each 2 hour 12 minute session). Sessions for Level III are not offered at the moment.

I failed CFA Level 1. What should I do first?

Take a week off, then read the score report properly and check when you can sit again under the attempt rules. Our step-by-step guide for candidates who failed Level I covers this, with a 20-week retake plan. Book a session if you want a second pair of eyes on your report.

When can I retake the CFA exam?

You may sit each level up to six times, at most twice in a calendar year, and not in consecutive windows or windows less than six months apart. The CFA exam dates 2027 page lists every window and registration deadline.

What time zone are the sessions in?

The calendar on this page shows every open time in your own time zone, alongside the time in Germany, so candidates in Asia, Europe, the Middle East, Africa and the Americas can pick a time that works.

What if I need to cancel?

Reply to your confirmation email up to 24 hours before the session and you can move it or get a full refund.
