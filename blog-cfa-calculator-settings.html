CFA calculator settings: set up your BA II Plus or HP 12C

Only two calculator families are allowed in the CFA exams. Three settings on the BA II Plus prevent the most common calculator mistakes; here are the exact keystrokes.

3 min read  /  By Gowtham Kumar

Your calculator is the one tool you take into the exam room, and it can cost you marks quietly. A wrong setting gives a confident wrong answer. Set it up once, practise with it every day, and you will not think about it on exam day.

Which calculators are allowed

Only two families are permitted:

  • Texas Instruments BA II Plus, including the BA II Plus Professional;
  • Hewlett Packard 12C, including the 12C Platinum and anniversary editions.

Any other model is confiscated. Most candidates choose the BA II Plus for its simpler worksheets; the HP 12C uses reverse Polish notation, which some people prefer.

BA II Plus: the three settings to make

  1. Show more decimals. Press 2ND, FORMAT, 9, ENTER. The display shows "DEC = 9" and the calculator shows as many decimals as it needs. Rounding too early causes small errors in multi-step questions.
  2. One payment per year. Press 2ND, P/Y, 1, ENTER. Some units default to 12 payments a year; with P/Y = 1 you control periods and rates yourself (divide the rate and multiply the periods for monthly problems).
  3. Normal order of operations. Press 2ND, FORMAT, scroll down until you see CHN, then press 2ND, ENTER so the display shows AOS. In chain mode, 2 + 3 × 4 gives 20; in AOS mode it correctly gives 14.

Press 2ND, CPT (QUIT) to leave the settings. They stay in memory when the calculator is turned off.

Habits that prevent mistakes

  • Clear the TVM worksheet before every time-value question: 2ND, FV (CLR TVM).
  • Clear the cash-flow worksheet before NPV or IRR: CF, 2ND, CE|C.
  • Check BGN or END. "BGN" on the display means annuity-due mode. Toggle with 2ND, PMT, 2ND, ENTER, then 2ND, CPT to quit.
  • Cash outflows are negative. A present value paid out is entered with +|−.
  • Reset if in doubt: 2ND, +|−, ENTER restores factory settings, after which you need to redo the three settings above.

HP 12C: quick setup

  • Decimals: press f then a digit (for example f 4) to show four decimals; internal precision is unaffected.
  • Begin or end mode: g 7 (BEG) or g 8 (END).
  • Clear financial registers: f FIN before each time-value question.
  • It uses reverse Polish notation: enter a number, press ENTER, then the second number and the operator.

On exam day

  • Bring both your calculator and a spare or fresh batteries.
  • Re-check the three settings the morning of the exam.
  • Practise every calculation on the same calculator you will use; speed comes from muscle memory.

Questions people ask

What calculator is allowed in the CFA exam?

Only the Texas Instruments BA II Plus (including the Professional) and the Hewlett Packard 12C (including the Platinum and anniversary editions).

Should I use the BA II Plus or the HP 12C?

Most candidates use the BA II Plus because its worksheets for time value, cash flows, bonds and statistics are easy to learn. If you already know the HP 12C well, keep it.

Can I bring two calculators?

Yes, a spare approved calculator is allowed. Check the current calculator policy before exam day.
