Pictures before formulas

Every lesson starts from what is true, builds the idea one step at a time, and only then writes the formula. By that point the formula just says what you can already see.

Take index concentration. The curriculum gives you HHI = Σ w² to memorise. Draw it instead, and it becomes something you will not forget.
