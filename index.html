Pictures before formulas

Every lesson starts from what is true and builds the idea one step at a time. The formula comes last, and by then it only says what you can already see.

Take concentration in a portfolio. On paper it is Σ w². Cut a square at each weight, along both edges, and the squares on the diagonal are that sum.

Weights 40, 25, 15, 12 and 8 percent. Squares so far: 0.000 of the board, so the portfolio behaves like ∞ equal-weighted holdings.
