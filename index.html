Three levels, one picture each

The CFA Program climbs in three steps. At the heart of each one is an idea you can draw.

Level I

The tools

Statistics, the time value of money and accounting. Toss a coin ten times, over and over, and the bell curve appears on its own.

Level II

Valuation

What is an asset worth today? Each future cash flow (outline) shrinks to its present value (filled), and the pieces add up to the price.

Level III

Portfolio management

Every dot is a possible portfolio. The best ones lie on the frontier, and the yellow line shows the best mix of all.

Pictures before formulas

Every lesson starts from what is true and builds the idea one step at a time. The formula comes last, and by then it only says what you can already see.

Take how concentrated a portfolio is. On paper it is Σ w². Cut a square at each weight, along both edges, and the squares on the diagonal are that sum.

Why options lose value as time passes

An option is worth its payoff plus time value. The same option is drawn a year, five months and one month before expiry: each curve sits closer to the dashed payoff, because there is less time left for the price to move.
