CFA exam day: what to bring and what to expect

Passport, approved calculator, arrive early, and expect strict security. A checklist so nothing on the day costs you marks.

Study strategy · 2 min read

The CFA exams are computer-based and taken at test centres. Nothing on the day should be a surprise.

Bring

  • A valid, original passport, unexpired and machine-readable, with the name matching your CFA account. Some countries allow alternatives, but they need approval well in advance.
  • An approved calculator: Texas Instruments BA II Plus (including the Professional), or the HP 12C family (Platinum, Platinum 25th anniversary, 30th anniversary, Prestige). Spare batteries help.
  • Glasses (without a case), if you need them.

Leave at home or in the locker

Phones, smartwatches and other watches, bags, wallets, notes, food. Rules on water, scratch paper and breaks are set by CFA Institute and the test centre, so read the current candidate guide before you go.

Timing

  • Arrive about 30 minutes early. Security checks take time, and late arrival can mean you are not admitted.
  • Each exam is two sessions with an optional break between them. The clock keeps running if you step out during a session.

On the screen

  • Use the flag feature to mark questions to revisit, and leave a few minutes at the end of each session to review them.
  • Never leave a question unanswered; there is no penalty for wrong answers.
  • At Level III, type short answers. The answer box does not reward length.

The day before

Do not learn new material. A light review of formulas and your error log, a calculator check, and an early night will help more.
